# Safety and quality gates

## Provenance

- Every source-specific claim has a source ID and locator.
- Cross-source synthesis distinguishes observation from new inference.
- Missing evidence is marked unknown rather than filled from model memory.

## Identity and originality

- Keep books separate unless the user explicitly declares one continuity.
- Do not retain long excerpts, reconstructed chapters, quote banks or distinctive phrasing.
- Do not request exact imitation of a living author; convert style to neutral craft variables.
- Keep distilled skills private unless licensing permits sharing.

## Consistency

- Timeline facts include when they are true.
- Character knowledge is tracked separately from reader knowledge.
- World rules record cost, boundary, exception and enforcement.
- Every setup/payoff pair records open, partially paid, paid or abandoned status.

## Final checks

Search generated files for large verbatim passages, unattributed findings, source-ID collisions,
broken links, missing selected dimensions and reference-canon contamination. Report unresolved
warnings.
